\begin{abstract}
Generating realistic synthetic multi-asset financial returns is essential for stress testing, scenario analysis, and risk management---applications that require a generator to behave sensibly across distinct macro regimes and to preserve the within-sector dependence structures that drive real portfolio risk.
We propose \textbf{SF-MarketGAN-R}, whose contributions are threefold. \emph{(i) Sector-augmented factor decomposition:} we combine 5 global PCA factors with 5 custom Fama--French-style stock industry factors, 4 S\&P GSCI commodity sub-index factors, and 2 bonds level/slope factors, then apply sequential Gram--Schmidt orthogonalisation and unit-variance standardisation, yielding 16 mutually orthogonal factors that specifically capture same-sector co-movement (e.g.\ Visa--Mastercard in \textsc{Finance}, Gold--Silver in \textsc{Precious}) missed by prior PCA-only designs. \emph{(ii) Factor-structured capacity restriction:} the generator fixes factor loadings to rolling OLS and residuals to a training-sample correlation Cholesky, restricting adversarial learning to macro-conditioned corrections on intercepts and volatilities. \emph{(iii) Regime-gated correction-magnitude schedule:} the $(\alpha, \sigma)$-correction envelope is gated by a VIX-aligned encoder so the model trusts OLS in calm regimes and expands under stress.
On a 60-asset universe (bonds, commodities, equities) with strictly lagged factor conditioning, SF-MarketGAN-R achieves out-of-sample correlation Frobenius $2.58$ against factor-bootstrap $3.36$ ($-23.3\%$) and a training-residual-Cholesky bootstrap baseline FB+shrunk $3.12$ ($-17.4\%$), winning $5$ of $6$ evaluation metrics. The improvement is structural: autocorrelation, volatility-clustering, and leverage-effect stylized-fact scores all drop by $10$--$13\%$ relative to both factor-bootstrap baselines, while same-sector pair rolling-correlation RMSE matches the best classical method on the Visa--Mastercard payments pair ($0.091$ vs FB+shrunk $0.084$) without degrading aggregate metrics.
We compare against $7$ classical and neural alternatives (factor bootstrap with and without shrinkage, DCC-GARCH, Student-$t$ copula, block bootstrap, MarketGAN-60, and our prior PCA-only variant) on six same-sector pairs and show that factor-bootstrap with an appropriately estimated residual Cholesky already matches a GAN's pair-correlation fidelity, so the GAN's incremental value is not on any single pair but on the \emph{structural} stylized-fact fit that no classical method reaches.
\end{abstract}
