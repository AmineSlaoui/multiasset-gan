\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented SF-MarketGAN-R, a factor-structured GAN for multi-asset synthetic financial data generation. Three coupled design choices drive the result: (i) a sector-augmented $K=16$ factor decomposition (5 global PCA + 2 bonds class-PCA + 5 Fama--French-style stock industry factors + 4 S\&P GSCI commodity sub-indices) processed with sequential Gram--Schmidt orthogonalisation and unit-variance standardisation; (ii) a factor-structured generator that restricts adversarial learning to macro-conditioned corrections on rolling-OLS intercepts and volatilities, takes factor loadings directly from OLS, and uses a fixed training-sample residual Cholesky; (iii) a VIX-aligned regime encoder that gates the correction-envelope so the generator trusts OLS in calm regimes and expands under stress.

Under strictly lagged factor conditioning, SF-MarketGAN-R achieves 2025 OOS correlation Frobenius $2.53$ against factor-bootstrap $3.36$ ($-24.6\%$) and shrunk-residual FB+shrunk $3.12$ ($-18.8\%$), winning 4 of 6 distributional metrics. The three structural stylized-fact scores (return autocorrelation, volatility clustering, leverage effect) each improve by $10$--$13\%$ relative to FB+shrunk---a gain that residual-Cholesky fitting alone cannot produce and that we identify as the non-redundant value added by the GAN component.

A focused pair-correlation baseline study shows that \emph{factor bootstrap with a correctly estimated residual Cholesky matches SF-MG-R on every same-sector pair within measurement noise}. The common assumption that a neural generator is necessary for preserving within-industry co-movement is therefore mistaken: what matters is correct estimation of the residual correlation structure, not whether that structure is fed through a neural network. The GAN's incremental value is visible on structural temporal metrics rather than on any single pair.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
(i) The residual Cholesky $L_\rho$ is fixed at its training-sample estimate; three attempts to make it trainable all degraded the model because the discriminator saturates for our factor-structured generator (Appendix~\ref{app:L_rho_ablation}). (ii) The sector taxonomy is US-centric; international extensions would need MSCI GICS labels and potentially more granular factors. (iii) Rolling-year and portfolio experiments under the sector-factor specification are left to a follow-up; the PCA-spec rolling-year evidence (tight OOS-CorrFrob across 2020--2025 stress regimes) is expected to transfer because the regime mechanism is unchanged.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Natural extensions: (i) a direct Frobenius regulariser on $L_\rho$ to make it trainable without drifting under adversarial noise; (ii) international sector taxonomies and a cross-regime transfer study probing the VIX-aligned encoder on shocks not summarised by VIX (e.g., credit-spread-driven stress); (iii) rolling-year tangency / mean-variance portfolio construction under the final sector specification.
